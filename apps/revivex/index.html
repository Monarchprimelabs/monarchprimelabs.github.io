About

ReviveX is a hybrid fitness tracker for lifting, running, conditioning, routines, and progress analytics, built to help everyday athletes rebuild the athlete inside them one session at a time. Training data stays on your device: no accounts, no cloud storage, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

What it does

  • Strength workout loggerStart an empty workout or a routine, pick from a 74-exercise library with original illustrations, log weight and reps per set, and get a rest timer, a plate calculator, last-session numbers under each exercise, and a PR celebration when you finish.
  • Routine builderCreate and edit routines with per-exercise target sets, reps, weight, and rest; start a workout from any routine, or turn a completed workout into a routine template in one tap.
  • Run tracking, outdoors or indoorsOutdoor runs use GPS for live distance, moving time, rolling and average pace, and auto-lap splits, with the route drawn on a map. Indoor mode is a timer-based treadmill log, and runs can also be logged manually.
  • Hybrid sessions and HYROX race simulationLog hybrid sessions segment by segment, or run the live race player: 8 stations in official order with Open/Pro and Men/Women load standards, a live race clock, per-segment timers, one-tap station completion, and splits.
  • Progress analyticsA weekly training summary across lifts, runs, and hybrid sessions with week-over-week deltas; PR history and per-exercise trends with estimated 1RM; training streaks and milestones; and a body weight log with trend.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect syncOptionally write workouts, runs, hybrid sessions, and body weight to Apple Health or Health Connect, import watch-recorded workouts, and see heart rate, active energy, and daily rings. Optional and revocable at any time.
  • Share cards and data exportCreate ReviveX-branded recap cards for any workout, run, or hybrid session and export them as images (GPS runs include the route trace); export your complete local dataset as a file from the Profile screen.
  • Local-first and privateYour profile, workouts, routines, runs, hybrid sessions, and body weight live only on your device. No sign-in is required, and your training data is never uploaded — share cards and exports go only where you send them.

Your data

  • All training data is stored only on your device. No accounts, no cloud storage, no analytics, no ads.
  • Location is used only during an active GPS run and is stored locally with that run. GPS is optional.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect access is optional: workouts, heart rate, active energy, daily steps, and (on Android) run distance, used only for in-app features, never sold or shared.
  • Share cards and exports are created only when you tap share or export.
  • In the native iOS build, run routes are drawn on an Apple Maps view, which loads map imagery from Apple.
