SanChart — Privacy

Your work records are not transmitted to or stored on SanChart-operated servers.

Last reviewed: 2 September 2026

At a glance

  • All records live in an on-device database. There is no SanChart account, no login, and no developer-operated user database.
  • No analytics or telemetry. No Firebase, Supabase, or any remote database.
  • Your work records are not transmitted to or stored on SanChart-operated servers. Optional backups are created and controlled by you.
  • The only permission declared is optional Face ID / Touch ID for the app lock: "SanChart can use Face ID to unlock the app. Your data stays on this device." No location, camera, contacts, or notification permissions.
  • Encrypted backup and restore are designed but not yet available; they arrive in a later update.
SanChart is in development. A full privacy policy will be published here before release.
