About

CDL Prep Pro is a focused study app for the CDL pre-trip inspection, air brake test, and in-cab safety check. Built for CDL students preparing for the road test, the scripts are word-for-word what your examiner expects to hear. The full Form A walkthrough, two quiz rounds, and the Acronym Cheat Sheet are always free; one $9.99 in-app purchase unlocks the complete study library with no subscription.

What it does

  • Pre-Trip InspectionWalk through every part with the exact verbal scripts you say to the examiner. Form A (front) and Form B (rear) covered in full.
  • Air Brake TestThe in-cab steps in order, with verbal scripts and pass criteria for each. The optional wheel-chock step appears when Include chocks is turned on in Profile.
  • In-Cab SafetySeat belt, emergency equipment, gauges, mirrors, wipers, heater and defrost. Every item, every script.
  • Acronym Cheat SheetPMS, CBB, ABC, SOR/ARM, BLUSH, LBS, I-D-C, DOG, and ALAMPS. Expand any one to see what it means and where to use it.
  • FlashcardsQuick acronym review with Hard / Good / Easy study controls.
  • Section QuizzesPick Pre-Trip, Air Brake, In-Cab, Road Test, Focused Review, or mix them all, with answer feedback after each question.
  • Progress TrackingStudy streak, questions answered, completed quizzes, and average score, stored on your device.
  • Dark ModeFor studying before a shift. Switch it on from Profile; the preference is saved on your device.
  • Search and Compare TrucksSearch inspection items, air brake steps, and acronyms from the home screen, plus a Training vs Test Day Truck reference for drivers who train on a conventional hood but test on a cab-over.
