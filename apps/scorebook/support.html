Scorebook — Support

Slow-pitch league scoring: one tap per at-bat, live for everyone who couldn't make the game.

Questions, feedback, or something not working right? Email monarchprimelabs@gmail.com — we read every message. Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

How do I sign in?

Enter your email address and Scorebook emails you a 6-digit code; enter the code and you're in. There is no password to create or remember. Your session stays signed in on the phone until you tap sign out.

What is a claim code, and what if I don't have one?

Your manager creates your roster spot before you ever install the app. A claim code links your account to that spot and to every stat already recorded under your name. Codes work once; if yours was lost or never arrived, your manager or league staff can issue a new one from the Rosters page. If you only want to watch games, tap Skip.

Does scoring work with no cell service at the field?

Yes. Every play is saved to an outbox on the phone the moment you tap it and sent in order when the connection returns, even if the app is closed in between. If the server has plays you don't, the app resyncs from the server log and tells you.

Why does Scorebook ask for camera or photo access?

Only for importing a paper scorebook. The feature is optional, needs the app connected to your league's backend, and is limited to managers, coaches, scorekeepers, and league staff. The photo is sent to the transcription service and is not stored by the app.

I scored a play wrong. How do I fix it?

During the game, open Undo: it lists the last five plays and voids the one you pick, then replays the game cleanly. After the game, a commissioner can open Game Review in the dashboard, void any event, and stats recompute automatically. Nothing is ever deleted; every fix is a new correcting entry.
