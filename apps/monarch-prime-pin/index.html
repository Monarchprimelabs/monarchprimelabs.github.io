About

Monarch Prime Pin is a private peptide tracking and site-rotation logbook built for clear, consistent recordkeeping. Research logs, schedules, inventory, and templates are stored locally on your device. Every amount, date, time, schedule, and note is entered by you — the app does not calculate, recommend, or prescribe anything. Start free with five saved records, then unlock Lifetime Pro once. No subscription.

What it does

  • Manual research logLog a compound, the amount in mg or mcg, the time, one or more sites on a front/back body map with 24 zones, symptom tags, weight, notes, and an optional progress photo. Edit or delete any record.
  • Site rotation heatmapThe dashboard body map colors each zone by how often you logged it recently, across 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, and all-time windows with a day scrubber. Color shows logged frequency only.
  • Complete history and calendarA searchable log with full record details for every user. Lifetime Pro adds a calendar view with backdated entries and a photos grid that compares two progress photos side by side.
  • Reports and shareable summaries (Pro)Monthly summaries, weekly frequency for the last eight weeks, weight trend, top compounds, symptom reports, and site usage — shared as text or PDF through the system share sheet.
  • Schedules and local reminders (Pro)Dated entries you create that repeat once, daily, or weekly, with an optional on-device reminder that repeats only the title, date, and time you entered. No push notifications.
  • Inventory and record templates (Pro)Track containers with quantity, dates, and a low-stock level, and save reusable templates for faster logging.
  • Concentration worksheet (Pro)Divides a total mass you enter by a liquid volume you enter and converts that volume into U-100 syringe marking references. It does not calculate, recommend, or prescribe amounts and is not connected to your records.
  • Privacy and app lockOptional Face ID or device-passcode lock, plus a privacy cover that hides record content in the app switcher. Free for everyone.
  • Backup, restore, and CSV exportExport everything as a single backup file you control and restore it on a new phone, or share your records as a CSV spreadsheet. Free for everyone.
  • Three languages, light and darkEnglish, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese, chosen automatically from your device language and changeable any time, with a dark or light theme.

Your data

  • All research logs, schedules, inventory, templates, and settings are stored on your device. No server is involved.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app never sends push notifications.
  • The sign-in screen asks for a name and email, but no cloud service is configured in the shipped app: nothing is verified or uploaded, and the profile is kept only on your phone.
  • Camera and photo library are used only when you attach a progress photo to a record. Photos stay inside the app on your device.
  • Notification permission is requested when you save a schedule entry with its Local reminder switch on. Reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Face ID or your device passcode is used only if you turn on the app lock; the app never handles biometric data itself.
  • Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app only receives a yes/no answer about whether it was bought.
  • Data leaves the phone only when you share it yourself: a backup file, a CSV export, a report, or a progress card.
  • Deleting your account in Settings erases the local profile and all records from the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.
