Monarch Prime Pin — Privacy Policy

Personal tracker by Monarch Prime Labs

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version: Monarch Prime Pin does not collect your data. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you log stays on your phone unless you choose to export, share or back it up yourself.

Information we collect

None. Monarch Prime Pin has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and we do not run a server for it. We do not receive your name, email address, device identifiers, location, usage statistics, health information, or any of the records you enter. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored by the app on your phone, in the app's own private storage:

  • your log entries (compound, amount, date, time, sites, side-effect notes, weight, notes and any progress photo you attach);
  • schedules, protocols, skipped doses, vials, inventory and record templates;
  • if you use Lab Results: the lab values you type in, your next lab date, and any lab reports you save (see below);
  • the name you enter on the welcome screen, used only to greet you;
  • your settings, such as language, theme, app lock, notification choices and onboarding answers, and the date of your last backup;
  • small counters the app keeps for itself: how many days you opened the app, entries logged, notification actions, and how often the Premium screen was shown or led to a purchase (shown to you in Settings as "Your activity"), plus counters that decide when to show Apple's rating prompt. These stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, which is why the app offers a backup you control. If your iPhone itself is backed up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the app's stored data in that device backup, under Apple's terms.

Welcome screen: no account

The first screen asks only for a name. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. The name is kept on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

Photos and camera

You can attach a progress photo to a log entry and, if you use Lab Results, photograph or pick pages of your own lab reports. The app asks for camera or photo-library access only when you choose one of these actions. Photos you attach are copied into the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded. You can revoke camera or photo access at any time in the iOS Settings app; the rest of the app keeps working.

Lab results and saved lab reports

If you use Lab Results, the values, units, reference ranges and notes you type in are stored on your phone. You can also save a lab report as you received it, as photos or a PDF chosen from your camera, photo library or Files. The app stores and displays these files; it does not read, scan or interpret their contents. PDFs open in a viewer inside the app that only displays files stored on your phone; links inside a PDF do not open web pages. Lab results and reports are never sent anywhere unless you share or back them up yourself.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads your body weight, body fat percentage and step count. If you also turn on Health trends, it reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, blood glucose, lean body mass and waist circumference. This data is read on your phone to show your own trends next to your records, on Home and in Reports. The app does not save a copy of your Health data; the one exception is a weight value, which is copied into a log entry only if you tap to use it there. Monarch Prime Pin never writes to Apple Health and never transmits Health data anywhere. In accordance with Apple's requirements, Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or sale, and is never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Health.

Notifications

All notifications are local: they are scheduled on your phone by the app. The app is built without push notifications, so no device token is created and we cannot send you messages. Depending on which features you use and which notifications you turn on, the app can remind you of schedule and protocol entries, an unlogged entry from today's plan, a vial running low, a restock date, a photo check-in, your lab date, and a weekly or monthly summary. Schedule reminders show the title you typed for the entry. The other reminders use fixed text and do not name a compound. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in the iOS Settings app.

App lock

If you turn on App Lock, the app asks iOS to verify you with Face ID or your device passcode before it opens, and hides its content in the app switcher. The check is performed entirely by iOS. The app stores only whether the lock is on; it never sees or stores biometric data.

Purchases and Apple services

Premium (a one-time purchase, not a subscription) and the earlier Lifetime Pro purchase are optional and are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see or store your payment information. The app asks Apple, through the App Store on your phone, which of these purchases exist on your Apple Account. It also reads the download record Apple keeps for the app (such as when you first downloaded it) to recognize people who bought the app when it was a paid download or who used it early, and to give them their founder benefits. That check happens on your phone. The result is saved on your phone, including a small marker in the iOS Keychain so it survives a reinstall; iOS can keep Keychain items after the app is deleted. The marker contains only that status, nothing about you or your records. A local record of your purchase status lets the app work offline.

At positive moments, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt, no more than occasionally. Whether you respond, and what you write, goes to Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app is not told.

The Premium screen has links to Apple's standard terms and to this policy. They open in your browser only if you tap them.

Sharing and export

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share it, using the system share sheet or the clipboard:

  • Backup file: one file with your records, schedules, protocols, vials, inventory, templates and lab results. You choose whether to include photos; if you do, progress photos and saved lab report files can be included, up to a size limit. The file is plain, readable text and is not password-protected, so keep it somewhere private. Purchase status is not included.
  • CSV exports: your saved records as a spreadsheet file, and, if you use Lab Results, your lab values as a separate file.
  • Monthly report: a PDF of that month's records, created on your phone.
  • Progress card: an image that shows counts and streaks only; it never includes compound names.
  • Lab report pages: a saved photo or PDF, if you choose to share it.
  • Concentration worksheet summary: copied to the clipboard.

Each of these is created on your phone and goes only where you send it. We have no access to any of them. If you save a file to iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that copy is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Delete All Data on This Phone (in Settings) cancels the app's scheduled notifications and removes your profile, records, schedules, protocols, vials, inventory, templates, onboarding status and progress photos from this phone, along with your lab results, lab date and saved lab report files if you use Lab Results. It keeps your settings, your onboarding answers, the app's own counters, your purchase and founder status, and the last-backup date. (In versions before 2.0, progress photo files could stay in the app's private storage until the app was deleted.)
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone, including photos. As noted above, iOS may keep the small founder marker in the Keychain.
  • Backup files, exports and reports you saved elsewhere are yours to delete from wherever you put them.

There is nothing on our side to delete, because nothing is sent to us.

Children

Monarch Prime Pin is not directed at children. The app's Legal section states that by using it you agree that you are 18 or older, and because the app collects no information, no personal information from children is ever gathered. As the Legal section also states:

"Monarch Prime Pin is a personal log. It records what you enter yourself: compounds, amounts, times, sites, notes and photos. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a medical device."

"Products sold as "for research use only" are not made or tested for human use, and their content and purity can vary."

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. Because the app collects nothing today, any future change that introduced data collection would be announced clearly in the app before it took effect.
