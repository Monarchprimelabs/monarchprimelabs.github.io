Monarch Prime Pin — Privacy

A local-first app. Your records stay on your phone.

Last reviewed: 2 September 2026

At a glance

  • All research logs, schedules, inventory, templates, and settings are stored on your device. No server is involved.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app never sends push notifications.
  • The sign-in screen asks for a name and email, but no cloud service is configured in the shipped app: nothing is verified or uploaded, and the profile is kept only on your phone.
  • Camera and photo library are used only when you attach a progress photo to a record. Photos stay inside the app on your device.
  • Notification permission is requested when you save a schedule entry with its Local reminder switch on. Reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Face ID or your device passcode is used only if you turn on the app lock; the app never handles biometric data itself.
  • Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app only receives a yes/no answer about whether it was bought.
  • Data leaves the phone only when you share it yourself: a backup file, a CSV export, a report, or a progress card.
  • Deleting your account in Settings erases the local profile and all records from the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.
This page summarizes how the app handles data. A full privacy policy for Monarch Prime Pin is being finalized and will be published here.
