How it works

Four moves. Zero clutter.

01

Log in seconds

Pick the compound, the amount you took, the time and the site on a front and back body map. Add symptoms, weight, notes or a progress photo if you want. "Log this again" repeats your last entry in one tap.

02

See your rotation

The body map colors every site by how often you used it recently, from rested to hot. At a glance you know which sites have had a break.

03

Reminders that work for you

Set a plan and get a reminder at the times you choose. Tap "Log it" right from the notification, or snooze it for an hour. Quiet hours keep nights quiet.

04

Look back with clarity

History, a calendar, photos and monthly summaries of your own entries. Export a CSV or a full backup whenever you like.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Logging is unlimited, and the body map, two plans with reminders, vials, calendar, photos, export and backup are free. Premium is optional.

Does the app tell me how much to take?

No. Monarch Prime Pin is a logbook. Every amount, date and plan is entered by you. It doesn't recommend, calculate or prescribe amounts. Talk to your clinician about your care.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. There's no account and no Monarch server holding your log. You can export a CSV or a backup file whenever you like.

Is there an Android version?

It's on the way to Google Play. iPhone and iPad are available now.

I bought Lifetime Pro. What happens?

You keep everything and get Premium free, for life. Nothing to do: it's recognized from your existing purchase.
